# Water Sort / Color Sort Puzzle

## Overview

A set of tubes, each partially filled with layered segments of colored liquid. Players pour liquid from one tube into another — the rule is that liquid can only be poured onto liquid of the same color (or into an empty tube). The goal is to sort all colors so each tube holds one pure color. Difficulty scales by increasing tube count, color mixing depth, and restricting the number of available empty tubes.

## Game Mechanics

- **Setup:** A grid of 4–12 tubes with 4 color segments each, colors mixed across tubes
- **Control:** Tap source tube, then tap destination tube to pour
- **Pour rule:** Liquid transfers only if the top color of the source matches the top color of the destination, or the destination is empty
- **Win condition:** Every tube contains a single pure color (or is empty)
- **Fail condition:** No valid moves remain (though most implementations allow unlimited undos)
- **Difficulty scaling:** More tubes, more colors, deeper mixed stacks, fewer empty buffer tubes

Ball Sort Puzzle is a mechanical sibling, replacing liquid with colored balls that pop into tubes one at a time — functionally identical.

## What Makes It Appealing

### Order from Chaos
The core emotional arc of Water Sort is transforming a chaotic, mixed-color state into a clean, separated state. This visual transformation — from messy to pristine — triggers the same reward circuitry as organizing a cluttered desk or sorting a pile of laundry. The "before" and "after" states are the entire pitch.

### Viscous Pour Animation
The liquid animation is inherently satisfying to watch. Smooth, slow, physics-consistent flow reads as tactile even through a screen. The moment a color "completes" — a full tube of a single hue — produces a short visual flash that functions as micro-reward feedback.

### Low-Arousal Relaxation
Unlike most mobile games, Water Sort is designed to *lower* stress rather than raise it. There are no timers in most implementations, no lives, no energy gates in the core loop. Players explicitly report using it to manage anxiety. This appeals strongly to adults 25–45 who do not identify as gamers.

### Honest Advertising
Water Sort is one of the few mobile game genres where ads are genuinely congruent with gameplay. What you see in the ad is what you get in the game, which contributes to better-than-average D7 retention for the genre.

## Psychological Principles at Play

| Principle | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **OCD-adjacent satisfaction** | Creating order from disorder mirrors organizational behavior; players with anxiety report therapeutic use |
| **Flow State** (Csikszentmihalyi) | Simple enough to avoid frustration, complex enough to require thought — sits in the sweet spot of the flow model |
| **Low-arousal relaxation** | Lowers cortisol rather than raising adrenaline; rare in mobile gaming and highly appealing to adult demographics |
| **Non-competitive framing** | No leaderboard, no opponents, no time pressure — pure personal achievement loop |
| **Near-completion pull** | Showing a nearly-solved board in ads activates completion drive more powerfully than showing the start state |

## Notable Games

- **Water Sort Puzzle** (IEC Global Pty Ltd) — over 100 million downloads; the genre's dominant title; accumulated ~44,000 ad creatives
- **Color Water Sort Puzzle** (PlaySpare) — close competitor
- **Ball Sort Puzzle** — ball-based mechanical variant; comparable download numbers

## Market Performance

Water Sort achieved a 12x increase in organic App Store visits after creative optimization. The game's sustained success through 2020–2024 is attributed partly to its genuine relaxation utility — players return because the game works as a stress management tool, not just a pastime. Retention curves are flatter than hyper-casual norms, suggesting a loyal core base rather than viral spikes.
